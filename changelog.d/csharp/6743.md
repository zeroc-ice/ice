- `IceSSL.CheckCRL=1` now checks the revocation status of the peer's own certificate only, and `IceSSL.CheckCRL=2`
  checks every certificate in the peer's chain except the root. Previously, `IceSSL.CheckCRL=1` also rejected a peer
  whose intermediate CA certificate was revoked. Both values still reject a certificate whose revocation status cannot
  be determined.
