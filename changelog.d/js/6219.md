- Fixed a bug where an invocation timeout greater than 2147483647 milliseconds — the largest value the
  `InvocationTimeout` proxy property can hold, since it is parsed as a 32-bit signed integer — made every invocation
  on the proxy fail with an `InvocationTimeoutException` after about a millisecond, instead of after the requested
  delay. Such a timeout is now rejected with a `RangeError`.
- The `ice_locatorCacheTimeout` and `ice_invocationTimeout` proxy methods now normalize their argument, matching the
  other mappings: any negative timeout means infinite and is normalized to -1, and a timeout that is not a whole
  number of the corresponding proxy property's unit is rounded up to the next whole number. Both methods now reject
  a timeout that is not a number, and a timeout greater than 2147483647 of the property's unit — 2147483647 seconds
  for `ice_locatorCacheTimeout`, which was previously accepted. Neither method rejects `0` or a negative timeout
  other than -1 any more.
