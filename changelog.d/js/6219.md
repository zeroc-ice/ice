- Fixed a bug where an invocation timeout greater than 2147483647 milliseconds made every invocation on the proxy
  fail with an `InvocationTimeoutException` after about a millisecond, instead of after the requested delay. Such a
  timeout is now rejected with a `RangeError`.
- The `ice_locatorCacheTimeout` and `ice_invocationTimeout` proxy methods now normalize their argument, matching the
  other mappings: any negative timeout means infinite and is normalized to -1, and a timeout that is not a whole
  number of the corresponding proxy property's unit is rounded up to the next whole number. These methods no longer
  reject `0` or negative timeouts other than -1, and they now reject a timeout that is not a number.
